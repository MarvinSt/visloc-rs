      How it works
      
      
      Pick a folder of photos of one scene and press Start run. The photos are matched, the
      cameras and a sparse point cloud are reconstructed with the COLMAP-port mapper, and
      (optionally) 3D Gaussian splats are trained on the GPU. Progress, the log and the
      reconstruction appear on the right; the outputs (scene.ply, mesh.ply,
      COLMAP text model) can be downloaded when the run ends.
      
      
      Runs one at a time. Everything stays on this machine.
